Track every application, interview and follow-up in one place.

JobTrack is a personal job-search workspace. It keeps your applications, interview rounds, deadlines and reminders organised so you always know the next step to take.

View the project

What JobTrack does

  • Application pipeline — record company, role, source, salary range and status from Saved to Offer.
  • Interview schedule — log each round with time, timezone, format and meeting link, and export an ICS calendar.
  • Deadline reminders — optional email alerts 7, 3 and 1 day before a deadline you set yourself.
  • Notes and next actions — private notes and a clear next action with an optional deadline.
  • Analytics — response rate, pipeline velocity and stage distribution for your own search.
  • Export and deletion — download everything as JSON or CSV, or delete the account and its data at any time.

Signing in with Google

You can create a JobTrack account with an email and password, or sign in with Google. When you choose Google, JobTrack only receives your verified email address and basic profile identifier from Google, and uses them to authenticate you and let you return to your account. It does not read your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or contacts.

Your data

Data is stored in the JobTrack application database and is visible only to your account. You can export it or delete it at any time from the Settings page in the app.
