Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

JobTrack ("the app", "we") is a personal job-application tracker. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, where it is stored, and how you can remove it.

1. Information we collect

CategoryWhat is collectedWhy
Account Email address; a password hash if you register with a password; otherwise an authentication identifier issued by Google. To create your account, sign you in and keep your data separate from other users.
Job-search data you enter Applications, companies, roles, sources, salary ranges, statuses, notes, next actions, deadlines, interview rounds, calendar links and contacts. To provide the tracking features you asked for.
Optional profile Name, phone number, CV link, portfolio link, skills and a short bio, if you choose to fill them in. To prefill forms and support CV and application features.
Technical logs Rate-limit counters keyed by a hashed client IP and hashed email, and authentication session records. To prevent abuse and keep your session secure.

2. Data received from Google

If you choose Continue with Google, Google sends us a signed token containing:

  • your email address, and whether that address is verified by Google;
  • a stable Google account identifier, used only to link your Google sign-in to your JobTrack account;
  • optionally your name and profile picture URL.

Limited use. JobTrack uses Google user data only to authenticate you and to display your account in the app. It does not read your Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Google Contacts or any other Google service, does not sell Google user data, and does not use it for advertising, profiling or training machine-learning models. JobTrack's use of data received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

JobTrack requests only the openid, email and profile scopes, which are not sensitive or restricted scopes.

3. How we use the information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your signed-in session.
  • To store and display the applications, interviews and notes you create.
  • To send optional reminder emails you enable for deadlines and interviews.
  • To protect the service from abuse through rate limiting and audit records.

We do not use your data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not share it with third parties except as described below.

4. Storage and security

  • Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database operated for JobTrack.
  • Sign-in sessions use an HttpOnly cookie containing a signed token; session tokens are never readable by page scripts.
  • Passwords are stored only as Argon2id hashes, never in plain text.
  • Google sign-in tokens are verified against Google's published signing keys before an account is created or linked.
  • Each record is scoped to your account, and server-side checks reject any request that tries to read or modify another user's data.

5. Email delivery

If you enable reminders, the app sends email through the configured mail provider. Reminder emails contain the company, role, deadline and next action of the application concerned. Reminders are optional and can be turned off in the app or per application.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Your data is retained while your account exists.
  • You can export all data at any time as JSON or CSV from the Data tab of the Settings page.
  • You can delete your account from the Security tab: this permanently deletes your account and all related records, including applications, interviews, notes, profile and OAuth links.
  • You can revoke JobTrack's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your information from within the app. If you cannot do so from the app, contact us at the address below and we will help.

8. Children

JobTrack is not directed to children under 16 and does not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and material changes will be announced in the app. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
